StickmanStudio Uploader ("the App") is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by its owner to upload the owner's own original animated short videos to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

  • The App is not offered to the public. Only the owner's own accounts are connected to it.

  • The App only acts after the owner authorizes it through TikTok's official login, and the owner can revoke that access at any time from TikTok's settings.

  • These terms may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

The App is used only by its owner. It does not collect data from other people.

  • Data accessed: basic profile information of the owner's TikTok account (such as display name and account ID) and the permission to upload videos, granted through TikTok Login Kit.
  • Use: this data is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's account.
  • Storage: access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared or sent to third parties.
  • Deletion: the owner can delete the local tokens at any time and revoke access in TikTok's settings under "Security and permissions".
  • Contact: questions can be sent through the contact email registered in the developer's TikTok for Developers profile.
